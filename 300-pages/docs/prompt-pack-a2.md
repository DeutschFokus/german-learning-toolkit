Copy and paste, in this order. Everything in a grey box is one prompt.

## Step 1 · Project instructions (once)

In ChatGPT: create a Project, open its instructions and paste this. It then applies to every picture.

```text
You are illustrating a German course book. Every image you make follows this style exactly:

Flat vector editorial illustration for a language course book. Bold simple geometric shapes, no outlines, no gradients, no texture, flat colour with at most one darker tone for shadows. Rounded forms; circles and arches in the background; generous empty areas; calm and friendly. People have simple faces with two dot eyes and a small smile, natural adult proportions, mitten-like hands, clothes in solid colours. Never draw any text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks: signs, screens and notes stay blank.

Base colours for every picture: navy #1B1E2F for the darkest details instead of black, cream #FBF4E6 and white for light. Each request names a few more palette colours – use only those plus the colours of the characters.
When I attach character sheets, the people must match them exactly: same hair, same skin tone, same top.
```
