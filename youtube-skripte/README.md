## How each script is laid out

Every scene has a number, an English image prompt in backticks, and the German narration as a quote block. Add the style line at the top of each script to every prompt so the look stays consistent. Each script also ends with Shorts cut-downs, a copy-paste description and a pinned comment.

## Production checklist

**Images:** 16:9 at 1920×1080 or larger. Show each image for 5–8 seconds with a slow zoom or pan (100 → 110 %). For scenes with long narration, generate a second variant (a closer crop or another angle) and cut between the two, so the picture changes every 5–7 seconds. That pacing is what holds retention in image-only videos.

**Voice:** calm German documentary voice, about 150 words/min. Check how the voice reads years and big numbers. If it reads „1324“ wrong, write it out („dreizehnhundertvierundzwanzig“) in the narration file.

**Sound, halal:** no instrumental music. Use sound design instead (wind, crowds, rain, fire, machines, clock ticks, whooshes on cuts), or a vocal-only background. Good sound design holds retention as well as music does.

**Images, halal and ad-friendly:** no depiction of the Prophet (Friede sei mit ihm) or the Sahaba. Modest clothing. No gore. No swastikas or Nazi symbols (German law plus YouTube ad rules). No AI-generated brand logos (overlay real logos in the edit if needed). Show real people only as silhouettes or from behind, never as fake photorealistic faces.

**YouTube rules:** turn on "altered or synthetic content" in YouTube Studio when realistic AI images show real people or events. YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" update demonetizes mass-produced templated AI videos. Original scripts, a real voice where possible and real editing keep you in the Partner Program.

**Subtitles:** burn in German captions, big, two lines max. They help retention, especially for viewers watching muted.

**Shorts:** each script lists 3–4 cut-downs. Generate those key images in 9:16 as well. Post 2–3 Shorts in the week after each long video, each ending with „Die ganze Geschichte findest du auf meinem Kanal“ and linking the long video as the related video.

**Upload order:** 01 → 04 → 02 → 03, one long video per week.
