# YouTube-Skripte: Edutainment auf Deutsch (halal, 16:9, nur Bilder)

Eight ready-to-produce scripts for a German faceless edutainment channel. Every one is halal (no riba, gambling, alcohol, music or indecency topics), has about 1,550–1,600 words of narration (10–11 minutes at a documentary pace of ≈150 words/min, still 9.5+ minutes with a fast voice), and is built for an image-only 16:9 video.

## Folder layout

- `0X-*.md` — the scripts: narration (quote blocks), numbered scenes with a short image idea, Shorts cut-downs, description, pinned comment.
- `sprechertext/` — the narration of each video with chapter headings and scene numbers [01], for recording with your own voice.
- `bildprompts/` — **full Copilot prompts for every scene**, a character bible and the step-by-step workflow for consistent characters. Start with [bildprompts/README.md](bildprompts/README.md).

## Production checklist

**Images:** use the full prompts in `bildprompts/`; never write your own short prompts, or the characters drift. 16:9 at 1920×1080 or larger. Show each image for 5–8 seconds with a slow zoom or pan (100 → 110 %). For scenes with long narration, generate a second variant (closer crop or another angle) so the picture changes every 5–7 seconds.

**Voice:** calm German documentary voice, about 150 words/min. Check how the voice reads years and big numbers; if it reads „1324“ wrong, write it out („dreizehnhundertvierundzwanzig“) in the narration file.

**Sound, halal:** no instrumental music. Use sound design instead (wind, crowds, rain, fire, machines, clock ticks, whooshes on cuts), or a vocal-only background.

**Images, halal and ad-friendly:** no depiction of the Prophet (Friede sei mit ihm) or the Sahaba, modest clothing, no gore, no swastikas or other extremist symbols, no AI-generated brand logos, real people never named in prompts. All of this is already built into every prompt.

**YouTube rules:** turn on "altered or synthetic content" in YouTube Studio when realistic AI images show real events or people. YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" update demonetizes mass-produced templated AI videos; original scripts, a real voice where possible and real editing keep you in the Partner Program.

**Subtitles:** burn in German captions, big, two lines max.

**Shorts:** each script lists 3–4 cut-downs. Post 2–3 Shorts in the week after each long video, each ending with „Die ganze Geschichte findest du auf meinem Kanal“ and linking the long video as the related video.

**Upload order (one long video per week):** 01 → 06 → 04 → 05 → 02 → 07 → 03 → 08. Publish 06 around the December timetable change or the holiday travel season and 08 in the first week of January.
