## Look of this video

**Main style:** Cinematic semi-realistic digital illustration with photographic lighting, contemporary Germany and Switzerland, overcast cool blue-grey palette with warm yellow station-light accents, realistic proportions, documentary framing.

**Alternative style `archival`** (scenes 04, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 19): 1990s archival documentary photo look with faded colors and soft film grain.

**Alternative style `painting`** (scenes 10): A 19th-century oil painting with visible brushstrokes, warm varnished colors and soft daylight.

**Video-specific rules:** No logos, no railway company branding, no readable signs, screens or timetables, no flags. No real, identifiable person.
