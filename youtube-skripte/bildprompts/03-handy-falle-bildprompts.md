## Look of this video

**Main style:** Cinematic semi-realistic digital illustration with photographic lighting, like a high-end animated documentary: deep blue and teal shadows with magenta and warm amber accents, shallow depth of field, soft bokeh, realistic proportions, modern 2010s–2020s settings, the same color grading in every image.

**Alternative style `retro`** (scenes 14, 15, 16): The same cinematic semi-realistic digital illustration, but set in a 1950s laboratory with warm sepia and faded green tones and soft film grain.

**Video-specific rules:** No app logos, no brand logos, no readable screen text or interface text. No real, identifiable person. Screens show only abstract colored shapes.
