## Look of this video

**Main style:** Cinematic digital oil painting with visible fine brushstrokes, like a high-end historical documentary illustration. Warm palette of gold, ochre, terracotta and deep indigo, soft volumetric sunlight, gentle desert haze, rich detail. Setting: the 14th-century Mali Empire and Mamluk Egypt, with historically inspired clothing and mud-brick and stone architecture. Dignified, respectful, epic mood.

**Alternative style `modern`** (scenes 34, 36, 37, 38, 39, 40, 41, 42, 43, 44, 47): The same cinematic digital oil painting style with visible fine brushstrokes, but a present-day setting (Mali, 2012–2013): dusty beige streets, cooler blue-grey shadows, warm low sunlight. Dignified, quiet, tense mood.

**Video-specific rules:** No blood, no gore, no weapons pointed at anyone. No depiction of the Prophet Muhammad or of his companions. No rings or necklaces on men.

## Characters and recurring elements

### The King

**Who / why this look:** Mansa Musa, emperor of Mali, around 45 at the time of his pilgrimage in 1324. No authentic portrait exists, so this is a fixed, dignified design. Signature: white robe with golden-yellow embroidery, tall white turban with one golden-yellow band, tall wooden staff with a small golden orb. White and gold belong to him only — nobody else in this video wears them.

**Appears in scenes:** 01, 02, 03, 09, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21, 22, 25, 28, 48

**Fixed description** (already pasted into every scene prompt — never change a word):

> The King: a West African emperor of about 45, tall and broad-shouldered, deep dark-brown skin with a warm undertone, an oval face with high cheekbones, calm dark-brown eyes, a broad straight nose, full lips and a short, neatly trimmed black beard with a few grey hairs at the chin. He wears a voluminous white cotton grand boubou robe with wide sleeves, embroidered with golden-yellow geometric patterns along the neckline, chest and hem, loose white trousers, a tall white wrapped turban with one narrow golden-yellow band, and simple brown leather sandals. He holds a tall dark wooden staff topped with a small golden orb. No rings, no necklaces. Calm, dignified, generous expression.
