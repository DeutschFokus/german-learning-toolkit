# Ideen-Backlog: neue Videos (halal, Edutainment, deutscher Markt)

Topics for the next scripts, ranked by how well they fit the channel: a strong German or Muslim-heritage angle, a story with a hero and a ticking clock, and a high-paying audience (history, business, health, science). Each hook is the possible first line of the video. **Check every fact again before writing the script** — the hooks are starting points, not final research.

## Story formula (what made 09–12 stronger)

1. **One hero the viewer follows**, not a list of facts.
2. **A ticking clock or a threat** (a ruler, the Stasi, a deadline, a disease).
3. **A twist in the middle**, right before the comment prompt.
4. **A last chapter that lands in the viewer's life** ("jedes Mal, wenn du …").
5. **A short, quotable title + a 1–2 word thumbnail text** („28 MINUTEN“, „7 MILLIONEN“).

## Halal check before writing

- No riba promotion (an inflation or business story is fine, investment advice with interest is not), no gambling, alcohol, music or indecency as the subject.
- No depiction of prophets or Sahaba, modest clothing, no gore, no extremist symbols, no brand logos, no readable text.
- Living or recent real people only from behind, in shadow, as silhouettes or as hands.
