# Series Bible – "Nachtschlüssel" (Continuity)

## Setting
Lindenburg: a small fictional German old town on a river with a harbour. Always night. Key places: Thomas's locksmith shop "Schlüsseldienst Berger" (the sign is never shown as text), Petra's apartment building, Leopold's sleeper-train hotel at the old station, Emre's bakery, the "Haus Abendrot" care home, the harbour, Radio Lindenburg, the police station, Viktor's practice in a villa.

## Overarching mystery
Seven years ago Thomas investigated a series of disappearances. All the missing people were patients of Dr. Viktor Lang, and each left a letter. Thomas got too close, and a report (signed by Ruth) accused him of tampering with evidence. He was suspended and now works as a locksmith. Every episode, a locked thing (door, safe, box, room) opens a piece of that old case.
