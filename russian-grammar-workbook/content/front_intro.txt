## Why this workbook exists
You already understand a great deal of Russian. You follow conversations, you read simple texts, and you can often say surprisingly complex things. What breaks down is **production**: the moment you build a sentence yourself, the endings, the cases, the prepositions and the verb forms start to wobble. Multiple-choice exercises do not fix this — you can usually recognise the right answer. You need to **generate** it.

So this workbook does one thing, {{TOTAL}} times: it puts you in front of a complete Russian sentence and asks you to make the grammatical decision yourself — the missing ending, the right preposition, the verb form, the aspect, the word order, the mistake hidden in the sentence.

## Built from your own mistakes
Every module was designed around errors you actually made in conversation. They are not presented once and forgotten; they come back again and again, in new sentences, other genders, other numbers and other cases:

!> с моя учитель || с моим учителем || every word of the phrase changes
!> пользуюсь ноутбук || пользуюсь ноутбуком || пользоваться чем?
!> помогаю мой друг || помогаю моему другу || помогать кому?
!> думаю о мой план || думаю о моём плане || о + prepositional
!> на этом вопрос || на этот вопрос || отвечать на + accusative
!> относится к китайский язык || относится к китайскому языку || к + dative
!> с преподавателем испанском || с преподавателем испанского языка || "of Spanish" → genitive
!> через два неделм || через две недели || неделя is feminine: две недели
!> у меня нет свободное время || у меня нет свободного времени || нет → genitive

The sentences themselves come from your world: teaching German online, students from Arabic-speaking countries, learning Russian and Chinese, laptops, phones and bad connections, the gym, friends, plans and travel — mixed with shops, restaurants, transport and home life, so that the grammar does not stay tied to one topic.

## The nine exercise types
Each exercise carries a small tag in the left margin that tells you what to do:

| Tag | What you do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ENDINGS | Fill in the missing endings. | Я живу в больш__ дом__. → большом доме |
| FORM | Put the dictionary form into the right form. | Я помогаю ___ (мой брат). → моему брату |
| PREP+CASE | Insert the preposition and the endings. | Я думаю ___ мо__ работ__. → о моей работе |
| VERB FORM | Conjugate the verb. | Я ___ (писать). → пишу |
| ASPECT | Choose perfective or imperfective and conjugate. | (читать / прочитать) |
| UNJUMBLE | Build a sentence from dictionary-form chunks. | я / помогать / мой / брат |
| CORRECT | Find and fix the stated number of errors. | с моя учитель → с моим учителем |
| TRANSFORM | Change number, tense, person or meaning. | singular → plural |
| MIXED | Several decisions in one sentence. | verbs and endings together |

## Who it is for
An adult learner at A1+ to early A2 in independent production — someone who understands more than they can reliably produce. The grammar stays within A1–A2: it goes deep rather than wide, because the goal is not to have seen every rule but to apply the essential ones automatically in real sentences.
