**Winner: #1, folded together with #5.** It is a 10/10 claim that sounds false but is literally measured; it is about the viewer's own body (highest personal pull); it escalates naturally from nanoseconds (your body) to microseconds (GPS) to years (Earth's core); and every step can be drawn with original motion graphics (a figure, clocks, a bent grid, a lab bench, an orbit, a cut-away planet). #10 was rejected because the evidence has been seriously challenged since 2023; #3 because the "photon age" figure ranges over an order of magnitude and is easy to misstate.

## 2. Story architecture

| Window | Job | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Extraordinary hook | First frame is already the full claim, a glowing figure standing on the planet, and two clocks probing head and feet. Narration starts at 0.08 s. |
| 3–12 s | Mystery → mechanism | "Not a metaphor." The claim is literal. Einstein: gravity slows time; the deeper in the well, the slower the clock. |
| 12–35 s | Escalating discoveries | 2010: lift an atomic clock 33 cm and it ticks faster (measured). GPS: +38 µs a day. Light covers 11 km in 38 µs, so every satellite is built to correct for Einstein. |
| 35–50 s | Revelation | Dive into a cut-away Earth: the center is about **2.5 years younger** than the surface. Biggest visual and sonic hit of the piece. |
| 50–60 s | Payoff + loop | Back to *you*: a few hundred billionths of a second over a lifetime. "Tiny. But real. Which means, right now…" → loops into "Your head is older than your feet." The last frame is the first frame. |

## 5. Loop design

Clock hands, tick pulses, scanlines, star twinkle and dust drift are all periodic over exactly 60 s, the last frame matches the first, the music ends on the dominant (A7sus4) that resolves into the opening D-minor boom, and reverb tails past 60 s are folded back onto the start. The final line ("Which means, right now…") completes itself with the first line.
