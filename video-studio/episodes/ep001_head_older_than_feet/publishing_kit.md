## Pinned comment (drives replies)

> Bonus: in 2022 a lab measured this across just **1 millimeter**. Which fact got you — the 33 cm clock, GPS, or the 2.5-year-younger core?

Ready answers for the questions people will ask:
- *"But gravity is zero at the center of the Earth!"* — The pull is zero there, but you're at the bottom of the gravity *well* (lowest potential). Time dilation depends on depth in the well, not on the local pull.
- *"Then astronauts age faster?"* — On the ISS their speed effect is bigger than the gravity effect, so they end up aging slightly *slower*. GPS orbits are much higher, so there gravity wins (+45 µs) over speed (−7 µs).
- *"Is 2.5 years measured?"* — No, it's a 2016 calculation from Earth's density profile; the effect itself is measured in labs and in GPS every day.
