## 5. Visual rules for this episode

- **Images do the beauty, the engine does the facts.** Anything that must be exact (the globe and India's path, the collision, numbers, labels, the name tag) is built by me, never generated.
- **One look:** steel-blue shadows, warm gold highlights, fine grain, the same LUMEN grade on every plate. 1924 shots get a sepia variant.
- **No faces, no remains.** Climbers stay tiny silhouettes; the boot shot shows only the boot.
- **Moves stay gentle** (depth plates look clean up to about ±25 px of parallax). Energy comes from cuts, light and sound instead.
- **Disclosure:** the AI shots look realistic, so tick YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" box and use TikTok's AI label.
