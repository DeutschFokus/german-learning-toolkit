# EP007 — Prompt pack (images for "There Are Seashells on Top of Everest")

Generate these in the tool you already use (Midjourney, Flux, ChatGPT, Gemini, Leonardo...). Every prompt ends with the same **style block** so all shots look like one film. Make 2–4 variations per shot and upload your favourite (or two, named `_a`, `_b`; I'll pick).

**Specs for every image:** vertical 9:16, as large as your tool allows (at least 1080×1920, ideally ~2000×3500), no text, no logos, no watermark, no visible faces. Keep the bottom quarter calm (captions and platform buttons sit there). Save as PNG or high-quality JPG named exactly as below.

**Style block** (paste at the end of every image prompt):

> cinematic documentary photograph, vertical 9:16 composition, natural light, deep steel-blue shadows with warm golden highlights, high dynamic range, crisp detail, subtle film grain, shot on a full-frame camera, no text, no watermark, no logo

Midjourney users: add ` --ar 9:16 --style raw` at the end. Other tools: start the prompt with "Vertical 9:16 image."

**Negative prompt** (for tools that have the field): `text, letters, watermark, logo, signature, faces, modern climbing gear, fish, people in foreground, cartoon, illustration, 3d render, oversaturated, blurry`

## Required (8 images)

### s01_summit — the hook (0:00–0:04, also the thumbnail and the last frame)
> The summit pyramid of Mount Everest at first light, seen from slightly below across the Northeast Ridge, a long plume of wind-blown snow streaming off the top into a deep blue sky, golden sunrise light grazing the rock and snow, layered grey limestone bands visible near the summit, clouds far below, a clear sky band above the peak for a headline, [style block]

Leave clear sky in the top third; I place the headline *behind* the peak. Check: one summit, no climbers, no flags.

### s02_fossil_rock — "full of fossil sea creatures" (0:04–0:08)
> Extreme macro photograph of weathered grey limestone on a mountain summit, dusted with fine snow and ice crystals, clearly embedded marine fossils: round crinoid stem discs, a small ribbed brachiopod shell, a fragment of a segmented trilobite, cold blue shadow, low golden sunlight raking across the surface, shallow depth of field, [style block]

Check: fossils look like *fragments in rock*, not polished museum specimens; no ammonites (too young), no modern seashells.

### s03_ancient_sea — "the floor of a shallow sea" (0:08–0:15)
> Underwater photograph of a shallow, sunlit Ordovician sea floor 450 million years ago, a garden of tall crinoids (sea lilies) swaying, several trilobites crawling over pale lime mud, small brachiopod shells, sponges and bryozoans, a long straight-shelled nautiloid (orthocone) gliding in the turquoise distance, sunbeams and caustic light patterns from the surface, [style block]

Check: **no fish, no turtles, no sharks, no modern coral reef, no starfish**. Trilobites need three lengthwise lobes and a segmented body.

### s05_folded_strata — "it slammed into Asia" (0:22–0:27)

### s06_himalaya_above_clouds — "rose nearly nine kilometers" (0:27–0:32)

Real-photo alternative (even better): a NASA astronaut photo of Everest from the ISS (images.nasa.gov, search "Everest", public domain), or a Pexels/Unsplash Everest photo with no people.
