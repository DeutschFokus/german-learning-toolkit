# Munition im Meer — pictures and video clips (16:9)

## Pictures only, or pictures turned into videos?

**The strongest option is a mix.** Make every shot as a picture first. Then turn only **11 key pictures** into short video clips (marked 🎬 below):

- **Pictures + my 3D camera moves** carry most of the video. They stay sharp and consistent, cost little, and I control every move and cut.
- **Video clips** go where real motion makes the difference: moving water, waves, sinking bombs, the phosphorus flame, and the first seconds of the hook. AI video costs much more per second, often warps details (faces, hands, metal parts), and 100+ clips for 9 minutes would be expensive and uneven.

So: 89 pictures, of which you animate 11 in Kling, Runway, Veo, Hailuo or similar (5 seconds each, 16:9). If you don't have a video tool, it still works with pictures only; I animate those 11 with extra effects.

## How to use

Copy each box into your image tool, one at a time. Every box is complete. Midjourney: add ` --ar 16:9 --style raw` at the end. ChatGPT / Gemini: paste as is.

**Rules:** wide 16:9, as big as possible, no text, no logos, no flags or symbols, no readable signs. People only **from behind, in shadow or out of focus**. The look: cold steel-blue sea, murky green underwater, rust orange, warm low sun on the beaches; 1945 scenes in black-and-white documentary style.

**For the video clips:** use the finished picture as the start frame, paste the motion text, 5 seconds, no camera shake, no new objects appearing. Save the clip with the same number (`03.mp4`).

**Start with the first-minute set: 01–14 and T1** (and the 🎬 clips 01, 03, 07, 09 if you have a video tool).
