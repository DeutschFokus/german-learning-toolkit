## 2. Story architecture

| Window | Job | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Hook | Frame 0: "THERE'S A H◉LE IN YOUR VISION / RIGHT NOW" (the O is a glowing iris), the blind-spot test card and a big glowing eye. Narration starts at 0.08 s. |
| 3–14 s | Mystery (the viewer's own) | Guided test: close left eye, stare at the +, bring the phone closer, the dot vanishes; a 2.2 s try-it pause with a heartbeat and "DID THE DOT VANISH?". |
| 14–44 s | Escalating discoveries | Why: no light sensors where the optic nerve leaves. The brain paints over the hole. Not the only edit: blinks deleted, eyes jump 3×/s (even now), blur cut, gaps covered by stretching the next image back in time. |
| 44–52 s | Revelation | The stopped-clock illusion: glance at a clock and the second hand seems to freeze — "It didn't. Your brain stretched it." |
| 52–60 s | Payoff + loop | "You're not seeing the world live. You're seeing an edit." LIVE badge struck → EDIT. "One so good, you never noticed the hole…" → the test card returns, identical to frame 0, giving a second chance to try. |

## 4. Captions

Same LUMEN style as EP001 (uppercase Inter Display Black 76 px at y = 1338, word pop, upcoming words dimmed). Accents: amber for the brain's additions (paints, stretching, stretched, edit), magenta for deletions (darkness, cuts/blur), cyan otherwise. The hook line has no caption: the headline is the caption. The test card sits at y = 760 and instructions end at y ≈ 1230, so captions never cover the test. `subtitles.srt` carries the closed captions.

## 5. Loop design

The last 0.7 s rebuilds the exact first frame (headline, test pair, eye). The eye's gaze cycles every 10 s and the iris pupil every 6 s, the star twinkle and dust are periodic over 60 s, the harmony ends on B7sus4 → E minor, and reverb tails fold onto the start. The final line ("…you never noticed the hole…") runs straight into "There's a hole in your vision. Right now."
