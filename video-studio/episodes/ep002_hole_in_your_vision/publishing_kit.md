## Pinned comment

> Did the dot vanish? If not: left eye closed, stare at the +, and move *slowly* between about 15 and 25 cm. Then glance at a clock with a second hand 👀

Ready answers:
- *"It didn't work."* — Make sure the **left** eye is closed and you keep staring at the + (not the dot). Larger phones need a little more distance.
- *"With both eyes open I see the dot."* — Correct: each eye covers the other's blind spot. The hole only shows with one eye.
- *"Is the clock thing real?"* — Yes, it's called chronostasis (Yarrow et al., Nature 2001). The leading explanation is that the brain extends the new image back to the start of the eye movement; researchers still debate the exact mechanism.
- *"Does the brain really edit out blinks?"* — Yes: visual sensitivity drops during blinks (blink suppression), which is why you don't see darkness ~15 times a minute.
