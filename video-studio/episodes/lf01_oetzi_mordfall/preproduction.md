## 1. Topic: Ötzi, told as a 5,300-year-old murder case

Why it works in the DACH region:
- **Local:** found by a couple from Nuremberg, in the Ötztal Alps, after a border dispute between Austria and Italy; he now lies in Bozen. Everyone in Germany, Austria and South Tyrol knows the name. Few know the details.
- **True crime:** a cold case with evidence, a timeline and a killer who got away. In DACH this is one of the strongest long-form formats.
- **Science twists every ~90 s:** copper axe → border measurement → 61 tattoos → pollen show his last 33 hours → the arrowhead found 10 years later → the 2023 DNA surprise (dark skin, bald, Anatolian ancestry) → the "curse", debunked.
- **Built for watch time:** each chapter opens a question that the next one answers ("remember the axe, it'll matter").

Other candidates considered: Kaspar Hauser (DNA 2024), the Amber Room, Göbekli Tepe. Ötzi wins on visuals (Alps, ice) and on the number of science twists.

## 2. Visual style: cinematic true-crime documentary (photoreal)

Recommended over cartoon for this video:
- AI image tools are strongest at photoreal landscapes, objects and atmosphere; cartoon characters change their look from picture to picture, which breaks a 9-minute story.
- True-crime and history documentaries in this look perform best on German YouTube for adult audiences.
- **No faces, ever:** Ötzi appears only from behind or as a silhouette. This keeps every shot consistent, avoids getting his face wrong, and sets up the DNA chapter ("he looked different than you think").

On top of your pictures I add:
- 3D camera moves on every picture
- a 3D map of the Alps with the find site, the border line (92.56 m) and his route through the Schnalstal
- a "case file" look: chapter cards, an evidence board, Polaroid frames with notes
- a drawn X-ray of the arrow and the artery
- a timeline (Ötzi vs pyramids vs Stonehenge) and a DNA bar (92 %)
- music and sound design
