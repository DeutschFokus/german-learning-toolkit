## 1. Topic selection

The brief for EP005 was "as close to 10/10 as possible". The previous reports showed what held scores back: a topic people half-know (Kola), a list-like middle, and stories that are *about* something rather than about the viewer. So this round weighted **Personal** (×2) next to curiosity, and required a payoff the viewer can feel *while watching*. Twelve candidates, scored 1–10:

| 1 | **Nuclear tests left a timestamp in your body: bomb carbon dates your cells (fat 10 %/yr, heart 1 %/yr, cortex never, lens core before birth)** | 10 | 9 | 10 | 10 | 9 | 10 | **68** |
| 2 | In the age of dinosaurs a year had ~372 days | 10 | 9 | 6 | 9 | 7 | 8 | 55 |

**Winner: #1, by a wide margin.** It is about *you* from the first line to the last. It has a dark-history twist (the scariest technology of the century became a clock), a clean causal chain, real data to show, and a revelation the viewer experiences in the moment: they are reading the video through the oldest part of their eye. It also turns the hook into the loop line.

## 2. Story architecture

| Window | Job | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Hook | Frame 0: "NUCLEAR TESTS / LEFT A TIMESTAMP / IN YOUR BODY" above a photographic-style eye, a mushroom cloud reflected in its cornea. A ring of seconds ticks round the iris on "timestamp". |
| 4–18 s | Mystery → the mechanism | Into the reflection: the tests. Carbon-14 in the air nearly doubles (×1.95, measured). The real bomb curve draws itself; 1963 test-ban treaty; the level falls year by year: every year has its own level. |
| 18–34 s | Discoveries | Air → leaf → cow → you. A cell divides; its new DNA locks in that year's level. Stockholm reads it back: a sample's level gives the year the cells were born; the curve curls into a clock. |
| 34–47 s | The answers | Fat cells ~10 % replaced a year; heart muscle ~1 %; cortex neurons never. |
| 47–60 s | Revelation + loop | The core of the eye lens (x-ray: growth rings like a tree's) was made before birth, and light from this very screen is passing through it right now. "And we can measure all of it because…" → loops into "Nuclear bomb tests left a timestamp in your body." |

## 4. New in the engine for this episode (reusable)

- `engine/eye.py` — a photographic-style eye close-up: iris built in polar space (wandering fibres, collarette, crypts, furrows, limbal ring), lit sclera with veins and lid shadow, tapered lashes, wet highlights, a corneal reflection slot for any scene, x-ray-able pupil.
- `engine/volume.py` — a ray-marched volumetric mushroom cloud (hundreds of soft puffs splatted into a 3D grid, rolling ring vortex, rising stem, base surge, self-shadowing, sky light, blackbody core); all motion repeats exactly over the loop.
- `engine/bio.py` — the real carbon-14 record (with a cartesian→clock morph), a backlit leaf, cell division, a 3D DNA helix, fat cells, an anatomical heart, a brain with folds, the lens with growth shells, hologram silhouettes.
- `engine/hud.py` — crisp UI-layer text/lines/cards that follow scene transitions (shared with EP004's approach).
- `engine/data/c14_nh1_monthly.txt` — Hua et al. (2022), NH zone 1 monthly atmospheric ¹⁴C.
