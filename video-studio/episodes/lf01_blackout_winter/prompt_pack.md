# Blackout video — picture list (16:9)

**How to use:** copy each box into your image tool, one at a time. Every box is complete. Midjourney: add ` --ar 16:9 --style raw` at the end. ChatGPT / Gemini: paste as is.

**Rules:** wide 16:9, as big as possible, no text, no logos, no readable signs. People are fine, but **from behind, in shadow or out of focus** (no clear faces). The look: dark, cold winter night, lit by flashlights, candles and phone screens.

**Pace:** a new picture every 3–5 s, about 3 s in the first minute → **85 pictures + thumbnail**. I add the clock, maps, graphs and the checklist myself.

**Start with the first-minute set: 01–15 and T1.** Send them in the chat with the number ("07") and I'll build the opening first so you can judge the look.
