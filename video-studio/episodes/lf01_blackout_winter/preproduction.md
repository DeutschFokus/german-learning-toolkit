## 1. Why this topic has the highest reach

- **It's about every viewer:** their flat, their heating, their phone, their money, their family. Everyone in DACH asks themselves "Würde ich das schaffen?"
- **It really happened in Germany, twice, very recently:** Berlin, September 2025 (about 60 hours) and January 2026 (several days, in frost). People remember the news but not the hour-by-hour story.
- **Perfect timing:** we publish in winter, when the topic feels closest.
- **Proven format:** "Was passiert, wenn … – Stunde für Stunde" keeps people watching because every step is a new countdown.
- **Shared by families:** practical tips at the end, plus "schick das jemandem, der nicht vorbereitet ist". Older viewers forward it in WhatsApp groups, which is how German videos jump from thousands to millions.
- **An open loop to the end:** "der gefährlichste Fehler" is promised in the hook and paid off at 8:05.

Honest note: 10 million is still exceptional for a new German channel. This topic has a much higher ceiling than Ötzi, and the packaging is built for it.

## 2. Visual style: realistic cinematic, "dark winter night"

- Photoreal images (generators are best at this), all in one look: cold blue darkness, warm candle and flashlight light.
- People only from behind or in shadow, so everything stays consistent.
- Pictures of the real Berlin events are AI illustrations and are labelled **"Symbolbild"** on screen.
- On top I add: a big **clock/countdown** per chapter ("STUNDE 1", "TAG 3"), a Germany map going dark, a phone battery bar (30 min), an indoor-temperature curve falling through the night, the 50-Hz grid explained as a simple animation, the 11.7-minute statistic, and an animated checklist.
- Pace: a new picture every 3–5 s, about 3 s in the first minute (97 pictures + my graphics).
