# Bahn video — picture list (16:9)

**How to use:** copy each box into your image tool, one at a time. Every box is complete. Midjourney: add ` --ar 16:9 --style raw` at the end. ChatGPT / Gemini: paste as is.

**Rules:** wide 16:9, as big as possible, no text, no logos, no readable signs or departure boards (I add all text myself). People only **from behind, in shadow or out of focus**. The look: realistic, cinematic, grey-blue German weather with warm station lights; the Swiss scenes bright and clean.

**Pace:** a new picture every 3–5 s, ~3 s in the first minute → **90 pictures + thumbnail**. Charts, maps, numbers and the clock graphics are mine.
