## 1. Why this topic

- **Everyone in Germany has an opinion about it.** That means comments, shares and a huge search volume all year.
- **Austrians and Swiss love it too:** the Swiss comparison and the Basel story bring in the whole DACH region.
- **A hook nobody expects:** "Die Schweiz lässt verspätete deutsche Züge nicht weiterfahren."
- **Surprises the viewer didn't know:** "on time" means up to 5:59 minutes late, and cancelled trains don't count at all.
- **Fair and constructive:** it doesn't bash railway workers. It explains three real causes, shows a solution that works (the Riedbahn), and ends with money-back tips people save and send on.
- **A comment magnet:** "Was war deine schlimmste Bahnfahrt?"
- Fits the creator's values: neutral topic, no music needed, people only from behind.

## 2. Visual style

Realistic, cinematic: grey-blue German weather with warm station lights; the Swiss scenes bright and clean as a contrast. On top I add:
- a big station-clock motif for chapter cards
- a "Pünktlichkeits-Tacho" (60.1 % → 58.7 %)
- the 6-minute vs 3-minute comparison as two clocks
- a cancelled train "vanishing" from the statistic
- a network-vs-traffic chart (track −17 %, traffic +29 %)
- domino delays travelling across a schematic map
- the report-card grades (3.0, signal boxes ≈ 4)
- per-capita coin stacks (222 / 359 / 594 €)
- the Riedbahn before/after
- an animated refund checklist

Pace: 3–5 s per picture, ~3 s in the first minute.
